Part 2: An Unanswered Message

Prompt

Describe a time when you sent a message or an email to someone but did not receive a reply.

You should say:

  • Who you sent the message or email to
  • What the message was about
  • How you sent it
  • How long you waited for a reply
  • And explain how you felt when you did not receive a response

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[Who & What it was about]
"I'd like to talk about a rather frustrating incident that occurred a few months ago involving my university professor. I was compiling data for my final dissertation, which was a pressing matter as the deadline was looming just a week away. I desperately needed him to clarify a complex theoretical framework we had discussed in passing during a seminar."

[How you sent it & Wait time]
"Given the academic context, I decided to send a formally structured email via the university's official portal. I meticulously proofread it, hit send on a Tuesday morning, and expected a prompt reply, knowing he was usually quite responsive. However, days trickled by with absolute radio silence. I ended up waiting for five agonizing days without a single word of acknowledgment."

[How you felt]
"Initially, I fell into a spiral of overthinking. Because the platform indicated that he had logged in, I felt like I was being deliberately left on read, or worse, essentially ghosted by my own mentor. It induced a significant amount of anxiety, leading me to second-guess if my email tone had been inadvertently disrespectful. Ultimately, it turned out that his inbox had crashed due to an IT glitch, and he had never received the message. When I found out, my frustration immediately dissolved into sheer relief, though the waiting period itself was a deeply nerve-wracking ordeal."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you usually expect people to reply quickly?
    "It depends on the medium. For formal emails, a 24-to-48-hour window is reasonable. However, for instant messaging apps, I confess I do anticipate a near-instantaneous response."
  • Have you ever forgotten to reply to someone's message?
    "Guilty as charged. I occasionally open a message while I'm preoccupied, mentally draft a response, and then completely forget to physically type and send it."

Section 1: Online Texting

Band 8.0+ Answer: "I would argue that the primary driver is the desire for asynchronous communication. Unlike a phone call, which demands immediate, undivided attention and can often feel intrusive, texting grants individuals the autonomy to process information and formulate a response at their own convenience. Furthermore, in our increasingly fast-paced society, texting is simply more efficient for relaying straightforward, logistical data without the obligation of engaging in mandatory small talk."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "The fundamental flaw of digital messaging is the complete absence of non-verbal cues. When we communicate face-to-face, vocal intonation, facial micro-expressions, and body language provide critical context. In a sterile text environment, a harmlessly sarcastic remark can easily be misinterpreted as a genuinely hostile insult. Although emojis attempt to bridge this gap, they often fall short of conveying complex, nuanced human emotions accurately."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "Categorically, no. While texting has undeniably revolutionized how we transmit factual information, an overreliance on screens severely hinders our ability to forge deep, empathetic bonds. Genuine emotional intimacy, conflict resolution, and complex negotiations inherently require the physical presence and immediate, synchronized feedback that only face-to-face interaction can provide. Texting is merely a supplementary tool, not a holistic substitute for human connection."

Section 2: Social Respect

While freedom of speech is a fundamental pillar of modern society, it is not a blanket excuse for spreading hate speech, defamation, or targeted cyberbullying. Individuals must be educated to understand that their digital footprint is permanent, and maintaining mutual decorum online is just as imperative as adhering to the law in physical society."

Section 3: Communication and Society

Band 8.0+ Answer: "The impact is profoundly paradoxical. On a logistical level, technology has undeniably eradicated geographical barriers, facilitating instantaneous, global connectivity that was inconceivable a century ago. However, on an emotional level, it has precipitated a state of paradoxical isolation. We are digitally hyper-connected, yet the pervasive distraction of smartphones during physical interactions has severely degraded the depth, quality, and undivided attention required for truly meaningful interpersonal communication."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "The cornerstone of successful conflict resolution lies not merely in articulating one's own grievances, but rather in the mastery of active listening. Individuals must momentarily suspend their defensive instincts and strive to genuinely comprehend the opposing party's underlying emotional triggers and rationales. By validating their perspective before proposing compromises, we de-escalate tension and shift the paradigm from an adversarial debate to a collaborative problem-solving endeavor."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "The ramifications are highly pervasive and deeply destructive. At a micro level, poor communication shatters interpersonal trust, leading to alienated families and toxic, unproductive workplaces. On a macro, societal scale, the inability to engage in nuanced, empathetic discourse drives individuals into polarized echo chambers. This fosters an environment rife with profound misunderstandings, ideological radicalization, and ultimately, severe social fragmentation."
