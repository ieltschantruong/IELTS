# Test 1: 
## Part 1: Introduction & Interview

### House & Apartment
- Do you live in a house or an apartment?
- What do you like most about where you live?
- How long have you lived there?
- Is there anything you would like to change about your home?
- Do you think you will live in the same place in the future?

### Places Where You Hang Out with Friends
- Where do you usually go when you meet your friends?
- How often do you spend time with your friends?
- Do you prefer meeting friends indoors or outdoors?
- Have your favorite places to hang out changed over the years?
- What makes a place suitable for socializing with friends?

### Leisure Time
- What do you usually do in your free time?
- Do you prefer spending your free time alone or with other people?
- Have your hobbies changed since you were a child?
- Is it important for people to have hobbies?
- Would you like to learn any new hobby in the future?

---

## Part 2: Individual Long Turn

### Describe a traditional product in your country.

You should say:

- what the product is
- where it comes from
- how it is made
- and explain why it is important in your country's culture

### Follow-up Questions
- How often do people use or buy this product nowadays?
- Is it popular among young people?
- Have there been any changes in how it is produced?
- Would you recommend it to foreign visitors? Why?

---

## Part 3: Discussion

### Traditional Products
- Why do traditional products remain popular in many countries?
- Do you think traditional products are more valuable than modern ones?
- What challenges do traditional products face today?
- Should governments do more to protect traditional products and crafts?

### Traditions and Modern Life
- How have traditions changed in your country over the last few decades?
- Do young people value traditions as much as older generations?
- Is globalization helping or harming local traditions?
- Can traditions survive in a rapidly changing society?

### Preserving Culture
- What is the best way to teach children about their cultural heritage?
- Should traditional festivals and customs be taught in schools?
- What role does technology play in preserving culture?
- How can countries encourage tourists to learn about local traditions rather than only visiting famous attractions?

### Band 8–9 Challenge Questions
- Some people argue that traditions limit progress. To what extent do you agree?
- How can a country balance economic development with the preservation of cultural identity?
- Why do some traditional products become international symbols of a country while others disappear?
- In the future, do you think cultural differences between countries will become smaller or larger? Why?

# Part 2: Individual Long Turn

## Describe a live sports event you watched before.

You should say:

- what the event was
- where and when you watched it
- who you watched it with
- what happened during the event

and explain why you enjoyed watching it.

### Follow-up Questions
- Do you often watch sports events?
- Would you prefer to watch sports live or on television?
- Have you ever attended a sports event in person?
- Would you like to watch more live sports events in the future?

# Part 3: Discussion

## Live Sports

- Why do many people enjoy watching live sports?
- What are the differences between watching sports live and watching them on television?
- Why do some sporting events attract huge audiences?
- How has technology changed the experience of watching sports?
- Do you think ticket prices for major sporting events are too expensive?
- What benefits do cities gain from hosting large sports events?

## Competition

- Is competition important for children?
- What can people learn from competing with others?
- Are people becoming more competitive nowadays?
- What are the advantages of competition in schools and workplaces?
- Can competition have negative effects?
- How can competition motivate people to improve themselves?
- Is cooperation more important than competition?

## Sports and Society

- Should governments invest more money in sports facilities?
- Why are some sports more popular than others?
- Do professional athletes make good role models?
- How can sports contribute to people's physical and mental health?
- Should schools place more emphasis on sports education?
- What is the impact of international sports competitions on a country?
- Do you think sports will become more or less important in the future?

## Band 8–9 Challenge Questions

- To what extent do you agree that modern sports have become more about business than athletic achievement?
- Should professional athletes be expected to behave responsibly outside of sports?
- How can governments balance funding between elite sports and community sports?
- Do international sporting events help promote peace and understanding between countries?
