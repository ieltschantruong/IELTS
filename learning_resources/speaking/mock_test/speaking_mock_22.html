Part 2: A Competition

Prompt

Describe a competition you attended.

You should say:

  • what the competition was
  • where and when it took place
  • how you felt during the competition
  • and explain whether you thought the competition was well organized.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[What it was, Where & When]
"I'd like to talk about a university-level debate competition that I participated in roughly two years ago. It was a regional championship hosted by my university in the main auditorium. The premise of the competition was to argue complex socio-economic policies against teams from other prominent institutions across the city."

[How you felt]
"The atmosphere was fiercely competitive. Before stepping onto the podium, the anticipation was incredibly nerve-wracking. My heart was pounding, and I was genuinely terrified of freezing up in front of the massive audience. However, the moment I began articulating my arguments, that anxiety rapidly morphed into an adrenaline rush. Engaging in rapid-fire rebuttals forced me to think on my feet, and eventually, I felt a profound sense of pride knowing I was able to outdo myself under pressure."

[Was it well organized?]
"Regarding the organization, I must commend the committee; it was flawlessly executed. Hosting hundreds of participants could easily become a logistical nightmare, but the schedules were strictly adhered to, the audio equipment functioned perfectly, and the adjudicators provided highly constructive feedback immediately after each round. This level of professionalism truly elevated the entire experience, making it a highly rewarding, albeit steep, learning curve."

Follow-up Questions

  • Did you win a prize?
    "My team managed to secure the runner-up position. While we didn't take home the championship trophy, the experience and networking opportunities were invaluable."
  • Would you like to participate in another competition?
    "Absolutely. Despite the stress, I find that structured competition is one of the most effective catalysts for personal and intellectual growth."
