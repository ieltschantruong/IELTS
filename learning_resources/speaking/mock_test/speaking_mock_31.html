Part 2: An Intelligent Person

Prompt

Describe an intelligent person.

You should say:

  • who the person is
  • what he or she does
  • why you think he or she is intelligent
  • and explain how you feel about him or her.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5 - 8.5)

[Who the person is & What he or she does]
"I'd like to talk about Dr. Linh, my former thesis supervisor at university. She's a mid-forties associate professor of economics who splits her time between teaching and advising government agencies on urban policy. Beyond her academic duties, she mentors a handful of postgraduates each year, which is how I came to know her."

[Why I think she is intelligent]
"What sets her apart is an exceptionally analytical mind. She can dissect a messy, data-heavy problem and pinpoint the flaw in an argument within minutes. She's also quick on the uptake: I could barely finish explaining my research when she'd already anticipated my conclusion and the loopholes in it. Yet her intelligence isn't confined to textbooks. She's remarkably resourceful, once rescuing my project by suggesting a free public dataset after our funding fell through. Driven by genuine intellectual curiosity, she still reads voraciously outside her field, and she's level-headed under pressure."

[How I feel about her]
"Honestly, I look up to her enormously. Despite her credentials she's wonderfully down-to-earth, patient with beginners and never condescending. Working with her made me more confident and curious, and I'm grateful that someone so sharp-witted invested her time in me."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you think intelligent people are always easy to get along with?
    "Not necessarily. Some are so absorbed in their own thinking that they come across as aloof. But in my experience the truly wise ones are humble listeners, because they know how much they still don't know."
  • Would you like to be as intelligent as her?
    "Of course, though I'd value her habits more than her IQ: relentless curiosity, calm under pressure and generosity with knowledge. Those are things anyone can work on."
