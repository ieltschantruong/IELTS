Part 2: A Smiling Occasion

Prompt

Describe an occasion when you saw a lot of people smiling.

You should say:

  • where it happened
  • when it happened
  • who the people were
  • what made them smile
  • and explain how you felt when you saw so many people smiling.

🔑 Suggested Vocabulary

Contagious joy /kənˈteɪ.dʒəs dʒɔɪ/ Niềm vui lây lan
Beaming with pride /ˈbiː.mɪŋ wɪð praɪd/ Rạng rỡ tự hào
Euphoric /juːˈfɒr.ɪk/ Bầu không khí hưng phấn
Unison /ˈjuː.nɪ.sən/ Sự đồng lòng, cùng lúc
Genuine delight /ˈdʒen.ju.ɪn dɪˈlaɪt/ Sự vui sướng chân thật
Etched in my memory /etʃt ɪn maɪ ˈmem.ər.i/ In đậm trong ký ức

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[Where & When it happened]
"I would love to talk about my university graduation ceremony, which took place roughly two years ago in the grand auditorium of my campus. It was an exceptionally vibrant summer morning, and the entire venue was packed to the rafters."

[Who the people were & What made them smile]
"The crowd comprised hundreds of my fellow graduates, alongside our professors and immensely proud families. What universally triggered the smiles wasn't just a single joke or event, but rather the collective realization of a shared milestone. The defining moment occurred right after the valedictorian finished her speech. Upon the final cue, every single graduate threw their graduation caps into the air in perfect unison. At that exact second, looking around the auditorium, I saw an ocean of faces beaming with pride and genuine delight. The students were relieved that years of grueling exams were finally over, while the parents were smiling out of pure, unadulterated pride for their children’s accomplishments."

[How you felt]
"Witnessing this phenomenon induced a deeply euphoric atmosphere. I felt an overwhelming sense of warmth because the contagious joy in the room was palpable. It served as a powerful reminder that despite the individualized struggles we all faced during our academic journey, the triumph was universally shared. That collective, radiant smile is forever etched in my memory."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you often see people smiling in your daily life?
    "Thankfully, yes. Despite the stresses of modern life, a quick smile is still a very common courtesy when interacting with cashiers or bumping into colleagues."
  • Did you smile as well on that occasion?
    "Absolutely! My cheeks actually hurt by the end of the day from smiling so much during the endless photo sessions with my family."
