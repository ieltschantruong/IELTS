Part 2: Helping a Stranger

Prompt

Describe a time when you helped a stranger.

You should say:

  • where and when it happened
  • who the person was
  • what kind of help you gave
  • and explain how you felt after helping this person.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[Where & When it happened]
"I would like to recount an incident that occurred about six months ago during a severely heavy downpour. I was navigating my way home from the office, driving through a rather dimly lit, suburban road on the outskirts of the city. The weather was absolutely atrocious, drastically reducing visibility."

[Who the person was & What kind of help you gave]
"As I was driving, I noticed an elderly foreign gentleman standing helplessly beside his rented scooter on the shoulder of the road. He looked completely disoriented and thoroughly soaked. Despite the rain, I felt a strong surge of compassion and pulled over to play the Good Samaritan. It turned out his tire had blown out, and his phone battery was dead, leaving him completely stranded in an unfamiliar area. Recognizing he was in dire straits, I invited him into my car to escape the rain. I used my own phone to call a mobile mechanic service and waited with him for nearly forty minutes. During that time, I offered him some dry tissues and hot tea from my thermos to help alleviate his shivering."

[How you felt after helping]
"Once the mechanic arrived and fixed his scooter, the gentleman was profusely grateful. As I drove away, I experienced an incredibly profound, fulfilling sensation. It wasn't about receiving praise; rather, it was the realization that a simple act of humanity could significantly alter someone's disastrous evening. It fundamentally reinforced my belief in the concept of paying it forward, and the sheer relief on his face made the entire detour completely worthwhile."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you often help strangers?
    "Whenever feasible, yes. While I remain cautious in certain situations for safety reasons, I rarely hesitate to offer directions or assist someone struggling with heavy luggage."
  • Have you ever received help from a stranger?
    "Absolutely. Years ago, I lost my wallet on a train, and a kind stranger actually tracked me down via social media to return it fully intact. It completely restored my faith in humanity."

However, I posit that the underlying motivation is largely irrelevant.
