Part 2: Overcoming a Difficulty

Prompt

Describe a person you know who did something difficult but succeeded.

You should say:

  • who this person is
  • what they did
  • why it was difficult
  • and explain how you felt about their success.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5 - 8.5)

[Who this person is & What they did]
"I would love to talk about a close university friend of mine, David. He recently managed to successfully launch an educational tech startup, which developed an app designed to help high school students prepare for university entrance exams. It's now heavily utilized across several schools in our city."

[Why it was difficult]
"What makes his achievement remarkable is that he faced seemingly insurmountable odds. Firstly, he embarked on this project right in the middle of a severe economic downturn, meaning securing venture capital was practically impossible. He had to be a total bootstrapper, working a grueling night job just to fund the server costs. Secondly, his academic background was in literature, not computer science. Therefore, he faced an incredibly steep learning curve, having to teach himself complex coding languages from scratch through online tutorials during his precious few hours of free time."

[How you felt about their success]
"When his app finally gained traction and he secured his first major school contract, I felt an overwhelming sense of pride and profound inspiration. Witnessing his unwavering dedication and his relentless pursuit of a goal, despite the lack of initial resources, completely shifted my own perspective on ambition. He managed to defy expectations entirely. His journey serves as a highly pivotal reminder to me that being resilient in the face of adversity is often more crucial than having innate, natural talent."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you often ask this person for advice?
    "Yes, frequently. Whenever I encounter professional setbacks, I consult him because he always offers highly pragmatic, solution-oriented perspectives."
  • Do you think you could do what he did?
    "To be candid, I doubt I possess the same level of sheer risk tolerance. I admire his entrepreneurial spirit, but I personally thrive better in more structured, predictable environments."
