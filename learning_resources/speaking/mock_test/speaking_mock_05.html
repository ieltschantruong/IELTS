Part 2: A Proud Family Member

Prompt

Describe a family member you felt proud of.

You should say:

  • Who this person is
  • What he or she did
  • When this happened
  • Why it made you feel proud
  • And explain how this person influenced you

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[Who it is & What they did]
"I would love to talk about my older sister, who is unequivocally the person I admire most in my family. I felt immensely proud of her when she successfully secured a full scholarship to pursue her Master’s degree in a highly prestigious university overseas. This wasn't just a regular academic achievement; she had to defy the odds to make it happen."

[When it happened & Why it made you proud]
"This pivotal moment occurred roughly two years ago. The reason it filled me with such profound pride is because I witnessed firsthand the grueling journey she undertook. At the time, she was juggling a demanding full-time job to support our family financially while simultaneously studying late into the night. Many people doubted her, suggesting she was aiming too high, but her unwavering resilience to overcome adversity was truly awe-inspiring."

[How this person influenced you]
"Her accomplishment fundamentally shifted my own mindset. She became my ultimate role model and a guiding light during moments when I feel demotivated. Watching her succeed taught me that relentless dedication and a focused vision can legitimately turn seemingly impossible dreams into reality."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you often talk to this family member?
    "Yes, almost daily. Despite the timezone difference, we always make a point to video call and update each other."
  • Have you ever told this person that you were proud of them?
    "Absolutely. Right after she received her acceptance letter, I made sure to verbally express how proud the entire family was of her sheer grit."

However, it's worth noting that if the family environment is toxic, it can have the exact opposite, detrimental effect on a person's happiness."

Career milestones or academic accolades can often feel incredibly hollow if you lack a meaningful social circle with whom to share those victories. Relationships provide an emotional depth and resilience that purely individualistic triumphs simply cannot replicate."

Section 3: Happiness at Different Stages of Life

Band 8.0+ Answer: "Undeniably. The conceptualization of happiness undergoes a massive evolution as we age. Children typically seek instant gratification through tangible things like new toys or sugary treats, driven by an innate simplicity. In stark contrast, adults derive happiness from vastly more complex, abstract sources. For an adult, true contentment usually stems from securing financial stability, achieving a fulfilling work-life balance, and nurturing the well-being of their dependents."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "This is often attributed to a psychological shift in perspective. Older generations have typically relinquished the burning need to constantly prove themselves against rigid societal metrics of success, which frees them from a tremendous amount of anxiety. They possess the wisdom to prioritize emotional tranquility and deeply value the present moment, whereas the youth are perpetually stressed about shaping their uncertain futures."
