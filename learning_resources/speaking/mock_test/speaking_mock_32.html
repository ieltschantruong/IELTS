Part 2: A Happy Event You Organized

Prompt

Describe a time when you organized a happy event successfully.

You should say:

  • what the event was
  • who you organized it for and who helped you
  • how you organized it
  • and explain why you think it was successful and how you felt about it.

Meticulous planning/məˈtɪk.jə.ləs ˈplæn.ɪŋ/Lên kế hoạch tỉ mỉ
Run smoothly/rʌn ˈsmuːð.li/Diễn ra suôn sẻ

Lively atmosphere/ˈlaɪv.li ˈæt.mə.sfɪər/Không khí sôi động
Sense of accomplishment/sens ɒv əˈkɒm.plɪʃ.mənt/Cảm giác thành tựu

Model Monologue (Band 7.5 - 8.5)

[The event & Who it was for]
"I'd like to talk about the surprise 60th birthday party I organised for my father last year. It was held at our family home, and around forty relatives and close friends came. My sister and two cousins helped me, and I wanted to throw a party that would truly move him."

[How I organised it]
"It started with meticulous planning: I drew up a guest list, booked a caterer and collected old photos for a slideshow. I delegated tasks to my siblings, with my sister handling decorations and my cousin the music. Naturally, there was a last-minute hiccup, as the cake arrived late, but we kept calm and filled the gap with a toast, so everything still ran smoothly."

[Why it was successful & How I felt]
"It was a success because dad was genuinely speechless and the whole evening had a lively atmosphere, with laughter and old stories. Afterwards, I felt a deep sense of accomplishment, and I still cherish the memories of his smile."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you enjoy organising events?
    "Yes, mostly, though it can be stressful. I enjoy seeing people happy, and planning is satisfying once you see everything come together."
  • Would you do it again?
    "Definitely. Next time I'd start earlier and keep a backup plan, since this experience taught me that flexibility is as vital as preparation."
