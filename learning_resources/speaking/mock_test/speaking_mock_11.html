Part 2: Change of Plans

Prompt

Describe a time when you had to change your plan.

You should say:

  • what your original plan was
  • why you had to change it
  • what you did instead
  • who was involved
  • and explain how you felt about changing your plan.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[What the original plan was & Who was involved]
"I'd like to talk about a memorable incident from last summer involving myself and three close university friends. We had meticulously organized a highly anticipated three-day coastal road trip. The itinerary was packed; we had booked a beachfront Airbnb and planned various outdoor activities, including snorkeling and hiking."

[Why you had to change it]
"Unfortunately, due to unforeseen circumstances, Mother Nature decided to throw a wrench in the works. Just twelve hours before our scheduled departure, the meteorological department issued a severe weather warning for an impending typhoon heading directly toward our destination. Driving would have been incredibly hazardous, and the authorities had already suspended all coastal activities."

[What you did instead]
"Realizing we couldn't proceed, we had to rapidly pivot. We managed to cancel the accommodation and, rather than wallowing in disappointment, we decided to improvise and host an indoor 'staycation' at my apartment. We ordered a massive feast of comfort food, built a makeshift fort in the living room, and spent the entire weekend binge-watching classic cinema and playing highly competitive board games."

[How you felt]
"Initially, I was profoundly frustrated and disheartened because we had been eagerly anticipating this getaway for months. However, the silver lining quickly emerged. Salvaging the situation required adaptability, and the resulting weekend ended up being remarkably intimate and deeply relaxing. It taught me that while meticulous planning is great, the true joy of spending time with friends relies on the company itself, not necessarily the exotic backdrop."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you often have to change your plans?
    "Not frequently, as I am quite thorough in my preparations. However, living in a dynamic environment, minor adjustments are sometimes inevitable."
  • Did you learn anything from this experience?
    "Yes, I realized the immense value of psychological flexibility. Clinging rigidly to a fractured plan only breeds resentment; adapting swiftly leads to unexpected joys."

Section 3: Planning in Different Situations

Band 8.0+ Answer: "For a state apparatus, strategic foresight is not optional; it is fundamental to socio-economic survival. Governments must rigorously plan infrastructural expansion, healthcare budgets, and energy transitions decades in advance. A lack of comprehensive macro-planning inevitably leads to systemic collapse, hyperinflation, and severe civil unrest when crises occur."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "It is the cornerstone of adult independence. In an era marked by immense economic volatility, proactive financial planning acts as a crucial buffer. By systematically allocating resources toward emergency funds and long-term investments, individuals immunize themselves against devastating debt and secure their future autonomy."
Band 8.0+ Answer: "The primary danger is creating a single point of catastrophic failure. When individuals or corporations entrust their entire logistical architecture to digital calendars and project management software, they become fatally vulnerable to server outages or cyber-attacks. An overreliance on algorithms can also severely degrade our organic, cognitive ability to navigate problems independently."

Section 4: Band 8–9 Challenge Questions

Band 9.0 Answer: "Meticulous preparation is undoubtedly a necessary precursor to success, but it is fundamentally insufficient on its own. A robust blueprint provides essential trajectory and minimizes preventable errors, effectively setting the stage. However, true success is often determined by dynamic execution—specifically, an individual's capacity to recognize when their original plan has become obsolete and possessing the audacity to pivot swiftly. Furthermore, one cannot entirely discount the profound influence of sheer timing and external, uncontrollable luck."
Band 9.0 Answer: "I strongly concur. Contemporary society operates under the oppressive illusion that every waking minute must be hyper-optimized for productivity. This relentless compulsion to schedule our professional and personal lives down to the micro-level actively eradicates serendipity. By effectively scheduling away our downtime, we not only invite chronic psychological burnout, but we also stifle the organic, unstructured daydreaming that serves as the genesis for profound human creativity."
