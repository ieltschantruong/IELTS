Part 2: A Shopping Problem

Prompt

Describe a problem you had while shopping.

You should say:

  • what you bought
  • where you bought it
  • what the problem was
  • and explain how you resolved the problem.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5+)

[What you bought & Where you bought it]
"I'd like to recount a rather frustrating ordeal I experienced about two months ago. I had been saving up to purchase a high-end pair of noise-canceling headphones, as I desperately needed them for my remote work. I decided to buy them from a very reputable, large-scale electronics retailer in the city center, assuming it would guarantee a smooth transaction."

[What the problem was]
"The purchase itself was fine, but the issue arose the moment I got home and unboxed the device. Upon testing them, I discovered that the right ear cup was completely dead—it produced absolutely no sound. I had essentially been sold defective merchandise. I felt a sudden pang of buyer's remorse mixed with sheer annoyance, especially considering the exorbitant price tag."

[How you resolved the problem]
"I immediately marched back to the store the very next morning with the receipt and the warranty card in hand. Initially, the customer service representative was quite dismissive. He cited a strict refund policy and suggested I send it directly to the manufacturer for repair, which would take weeks. However, I had to stand my ground. I calmly but firmly demonstrated the fault on the spot and insisted that, under consumer protection laws, I was entitled to an immediate replacement for an item dead on arrival. Realizing I wouldn't back down, he consulted his manager, who eventually authorized a brand-new replacement. Despite the initial friction, I was deeply relieved that we reached an amicable resolution."

Follow-up Questions

  • Do you often return things you buy?
    "Very rarely. I'm usually quite meticulous about checking product reviews before purchasing. This was a rare exception where the defect was entirely unpredictable."
  • Did this experience stop you from shopping at that store?
    "While it definitely tarnished my trust initially, the fact that the manager eventually honored the replacement restored my faith somewhat, though I will be much more cautious in the future."

Band 8.0+ Answer: "Unquestionably. The digital age has drastically recalibrated our expectations. Section 2: Online Shopping Issues

Band 8.0+ Answer: "The most prevalent issue is a severe discrepancy between expectation and reality. Band 8.0+ Answer: "Modern marketing utilizes highly sophisticated psychological profiling. By leveraging algorithmic data, advertisers hyper-target our deepest insecurities and manufacture artificial scarcity.
