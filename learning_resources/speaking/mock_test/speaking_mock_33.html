Part 2: An Interview with a Famous Person

Prompt

Describe a time when you interviewed a famous person.

You should say:

  • who the famous person was
  • when and where you interviewed this person
  • what you talked about during the interview
  • and explain how you felt about the experience.

Model Monologue (Band 7.5 - 8.5)

[Who the person was & When and where]
"I'd like to talk about the time I interviewed a bestselling author, whom I'll call Ms. Hanh, for my university magazine. It took place last spring in the library's reading room, after she gave a guest lecture on campus. As the magazine's youngest reporter, I couldn't believe I was chosen to conduct an interview with someone so well known."

[What we talked about]
"I'd spent a week preparing, so I started with an easy question to break the ice. Gradually, she began to open up about her struggles early on, including rejection letters from fifteen publishers. We also discussed her writing routine, such as waking at five to write before work, and her advice to young writers, namely to read widely and never fear failure. She was remarkably candid, and her answers were consistently insightful."

[How I felt]
"Beforehand, I had butterflies in my stomach and felt completely star-struck, but her warmth quickly put me at ease. Afterwards, I was over the moon. It was a once-in-a-lifetime experience that taught me that listening carefully matters more than asking clever questions."

Follow-up Questions

  • Would you like to interview another famous person?
    "Definitely. I'd love to speak with a scientist about their discoveries, since I think the stories behind great achievements are the most inspiring."
  • What did you learn from this experience?
    "Mainly that preparation builds confidence. Knowing my questions in advance helped me stay calm and follow the conversation naturally."
