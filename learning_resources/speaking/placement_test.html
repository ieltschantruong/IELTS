Official Assessment Criteria

Fluency and Coherence (FC)

Speak dynamically with connected vocabulary and transitional keywords.

Lexical Resource (LR)

Avoid excessive repetition. Leverage collocations and idioms appropriate for the targeted Band.

Grammatical Range (GRA)

Incorporate complex compound structures with limited error margins.

Pronunciation (PR)

Enunciate clearly, maintain steady intonation levels, and split focus accurately across clauses.
