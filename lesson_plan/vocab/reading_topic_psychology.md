### **4.1. Cognitive**

* **IPA & Audio:** /ˈkɒɡ.nə.tɪv/ 🔊 \[Play Audio\]  
* **Part of Speech:** adjective (adj.)  
* **CEFR Level:** C1  
* **Definition:** Connected with thinking or conscious mental processes.  
* **Vietnamese Translation:** (Thuộc về) nhận thức  
* **Usage Explain:** The most fundamental word for psychology passages, relating to memory, learning, brain function, and child development.  
* **Example sentence:** "Playing musical instruments has been shown to improve **cognitive** function in older adults."  
* **Word Family:** cognition (n.), cognitively (adv.)
