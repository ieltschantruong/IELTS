# **IELTS Academic Reading: Vocabulary Data Schema**

**Topic 1: Biology & Environment**

**Curated by: IELTS Chan Truong**

### **1.1. Biodiversity**

* **IPA & Audio:** /ˌbaɪ.əʊ.daɪˈvɜː.sə.ti/ 🔊 \[Play Audio\]  
* **Part of Speech:** noun (n.)  
* **CEFR Level:** C1  
* **Definition:** The number and types of plants and animals that exist in a particular area or in the world generally.  
* **Vietnamese Translation:** Đa dạng sinh học  
* **Usage Explain:** Extremely common in passages discussing conservation, ecological balance, or the impact of climate change on habitats. Often paired with "loss of", "preserve", or "marine".  
* **Example sentence:** "The construction of the new dam poses a significant threat to the rich **biodiversity** of the river valley."  
* **Word Family:** diverse (adj.), diversify (v.), diversification (n.)

### **1.2. Mitigate**

* **IPA & Audio:** /ˈmɪt.ɪ.ɡeɪt/ 🔊 \[Play Audio\]  
* **Part of Speech:** verb (v.)  
* **CEFR Level:** C1  
* **Definition:** To make something less severe, harmful, or painful.  
* **Vietnamese Translation:** Giảm nhẹ, làm dịu bớt  
* **Usage Explain:** Used in problem-solution passages. It frequently appears alongside words like "effects," "impact," "damage," or "risk."  
* **Example sentence:** "Governments must implement stricter carbon emission policies to **mitigate** the effects of global warming."  
* **Word Family:** mitigation (n.), unmitigated (adj.)

### **1.3. Proliferation**
