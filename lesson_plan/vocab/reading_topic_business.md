# **IELTS Vocabulary: Business & Economics**

**Topic 5: Linguistics & Education**  
**Curated by: IELTS Chan Truong**

### **1\. Lucrative**

* **Word/Phrase**: Lucrative  
* **IPA & Audio**: /ˈluː.krə.tɪv/ 🔊  
* **Part of Speech**: adj.  
* **CEFR Level**: C1  
* **Definition (English)**: (Especially of a business, job, or activity) producing a lot of money.  
* **Vietnamese Translation**: Sinh lời, béo bở  
* **Usage Explain**: Common in texts discussing international trade, corporate expansion, or profitable industries like global tourism.  
* **Example Sentence**: The export of raw minerals proved to be a highly lucrative enterprise for the developing nation.  
* **Word Family**: lucratively (adv.), lucrativeness (n.)

### **2\. Fluctuation**

* **Word/Phrase**: Fluctuation  
* **IPA & Audio**: /ˌflʌk.tʃuˈeɪ.ʃən/ 🔊  
* **Part of Speech**: n.  
* **CEFR Level**: C1  
* **Definition (English)**: A situation in which prices, levels, or interest rates go up and down.  
* **Vietnamese Translation**: Sự biến động, dao động  
* **Usage Explain**: Used extensively in economic texts detailing market trends, and incredibly useful for IELTS Writing Task 1\.  
* **Example Sentence**: Companies must maintain cash reserves to survive the unpredictable fluctuations of the global market.  
* **Word Family**: fluctuate (v.)

### **3\. Monopoly**

* **Word/Phrase**: Monopoly  
* **IPA & Audio**: /məˈnɒp.əl.i/ 🔊  
* **Part of Speech**: n.  
* **CEFR Level**: C1  
* **Definition (English)**: (An organization or group that has) complete control of something, especially an area of business, so that others have no share.  
* **Vietnamese Translation**: Độc quyền  
* **Usage Explain**: Found in historical economic passages (e.g., the East India Company) or discussions on modern tech giants.  
* **Example Sentence**: For decades, the state-owned telecommunications company held a strict monopoly over the national network.  
* **Word Family**: monopolize (v.), monopolistic (adj.)
