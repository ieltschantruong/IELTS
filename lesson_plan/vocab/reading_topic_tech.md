### **3.1. Obsolete**

* **IPA & Audio:** /ˌɒb.səlˈiːt/ 🔊 \[Play Audio\]  
* **Part of Speech:** adjective (adj.)  
* **CEFR Level:** C1  
* **Definition:** Not in use any more, having been replaced by something newer and better or more fashionable.  
* **Vietnamese Translation:** Lỗi thời, không còn sử dụng
