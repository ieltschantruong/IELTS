### **2.1. Excavation**

* **IPA & Audio:** /ˌek.skəˈveɪ.ʃən/ 🔊 \[Play Audio\]  
* **Part of Speech:** noun (n.)  
* **CEFR Level:** C1  
* **Definition:** The act of digging, especially when looking for old objects, buildings, etc., in order to discover things about the past.  
* **Vietnamese Translation:** Sự khai quật  
* **Usage Explain:** A core word for any archaeology or ancient history passage. Often associated with "site," "uncover," and "artifacts."  
* **Example sentence:** "Recent **excavations** in the valley have revealed the foundations of a previously unknown Roman settlement."  
* **Word Family:** excavate (v.), excavator (n.)
